Chapter 3 -The Unexpected Challenge

After taking her first steps into the world of technology , Nova became more excited to learn.

One day,she decided to create a simple webpage using HTML. She opened her laptop ,wrote some code ,and eagerly clicked the preview button.

But something unexpected happened. Her webpage didn't look the way she had imagined.

The image was missing, and some of the text wasn't appearing correctly.

Nova looked at the screen and wondered: "what went wrong? Did I make a mistake in my code?"

For a moment,she felt confused.But instead of giving up,she became curious.

She opened her notebook and wrote: "Every problem has a question waiting to be answered."

She started checking her code, one line at a time.

She checked the image filename. She checked the HTML tags. She checked the links between herfiles.

Every mistake was becoming a new question.

After a few minutes, Nova noticed something unusual.

"wait... could this tiny mistake be the reason?"

Nova smiled and said: "Maybe this challege is another opportunity to learn something new!"

With her notebook beside her and curiousity in her heart, she prepared to solve the mystery.

Nova's Thought

"Every Challenge is an opportunity to learn!"
