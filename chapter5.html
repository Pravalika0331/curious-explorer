Chapter 5: The Creation

After solving the mystery, Nova felt more confident. She realized that every mistake could teach her something valuable.

She decided to create a webpage of her own. This time, she planned everything carefully.

She added a heading, some paragraphs, an image and links to connect different pages.

When she opened her webpage, everything appeared as she expected.

Nova smiled proudly. Her first small creation was finally ready!

She understood that learning to code was not about being perfect. It was about trying, learning, and improving every day.

Nova's Lesson

"Every creation begins with a curious mind!"
