Chapter 4: The Discovery

Nova carefully examined her HTML code again. She compared the image filename with the name of actual file.

Sudden, she noticed the mistake. She had written the image filename incorrectly.

"That's why the image wasn't appearing!" Nova exclaimed.

She corrected the filename and refreshed the webpage.

This time, the image apeared perfectly on the screen.

Nova felt excited. She had solved the problem by observing carefully and thinking logically.

She wrote another sentence in her notebook: "Small mistakes can teach us big lessons."

Nova's Discovery

Debugging means finding and correcting mistakes in a program.
